About

Smart Irrigation Using IoT is an innovative agricultural solution that uses Internet of Things technology to manage water efficiently.
It uses soil moisture sensors to monitor the water level in the soil and helps supply water to crops
whenever needed.
This technology reduces water wastage, saves time, minimizes
manual effort, and supports healthy crop growth.
By combining smart sensors and automated irrigation systems, farmers can make better decisions and move towards sustainable farming.
